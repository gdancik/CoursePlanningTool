<p>
It is important to articulate your grading policies around late work or make-up work, re-grades, and 
attendance and/or participation are essential. Please articulate in the space below your:
</p>
<br/>
<p> <b>Late Work Policy</b>: Will you accept late work? If so, until when? Will you deduct
points for late work, and if so, how many? Will you require any sort of documentation 
(e.g., doctor's note)?
</p>
<br/>
<p>
<b>Re-grade or Re-submission Policy</b>: If students ask you to re-grade their work or submit
a new draft of the work, will you? If so, how should they request a re-grade or re-submit?
(in writing, in office hours, within one week of receiving the graded assignment?)
</p>
<br/>
<p>
<b>Attendance and/or participation policy</b>: Is attendance and/or participation a graded component of
your course? If so, how will you measure student performance (e.g., a sign-in sheet, a tally sheet,
etc.)? Articulating what counts as excused or unexcused absences can help students
understand attendance expectations. By law, we are required to excuse absences that students may have due to observation of
a religious holiday.
</p>

</p>
