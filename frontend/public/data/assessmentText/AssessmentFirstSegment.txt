Faculty give students assignments for a variety of purposes, such as 
determining how students are learning, identifying areas where they can 
provide students with support, and allowing students to practice skills, 
build toward larger projects, and/or demonstrate their learning. 
Sometimes assignments are designed to meet multiple goals. This section of the course planning tool helps you to think about your 
goals for assignments and how to schedule them in your course to create 
alignment between the work students are doing and learning outcomes of the 
course.
