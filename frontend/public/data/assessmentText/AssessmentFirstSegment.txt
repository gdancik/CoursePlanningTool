As faculty assign work to students to identify how they are learning, 
identify areas in which faculty can support students who are having trouble 
with learning outcomes, allow students to practice skills, build toward 
larger projects, and to demonstrate their learning over an instructional 
unit or semester. Sometimes we create assignments that meet more than one goal. 
This section of the course planning tool helps you to think about your 
goals for assignments and how to schedule them in your course to create 
alignment between the work students are doing and learning outcomes of the 
course.
