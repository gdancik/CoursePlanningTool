It can be a challenge to ensure that your course schedule communicates your chosen learning 
outcomes to students. One way to reinforce the learning outcomes' connections to the course
materials is to map each meeting or unit with the corresponding learning outcomes. Planning
your class meetings and assignments while referencing the learning outcomes can also help you 
to examine the way learning outcomes are attained across the course and to make any necessary
adjustments to meet the goals you have defined for the course. For instance, you may realize
you have focused more heavily on communication or creativity and want to enhance engagement
with ethical reasoning. Making the explicit connection between the class meeting, learning
outcome, and assignments will reveal places where you may want to go back and add readings
or assignments that address certain outcomes.
